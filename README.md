# Yamaha Projects Dashboard

Performance review for seven Yamaha programs. Open `index.html` in a browser. No install or server is required.

The page uses the Yamaha wordmark and a white layout. Tie-Up, Local Mechanics, Pulse Card, Influencer, and Kizuna use sample data for review. Exchange and Apparels use the figures from their review reports.

## Programs

| Tab | What it reviews |
| --- | --- |
| Tie-Up | Partner placements and co-branded activity |
| Local Mechanics | Workshop jobs and genuine-parts offtake |
| Pulse Card | Card issuance, redemption, and outlet activity |
| Influencer | Creator delivery and campaign results |
| Kizuna | Member attendance and chapter activity |
| Exchange | Bike exchange quantity, brand and model mix, platform, and dealer ranks |
| Apparels | Apparel retail, dealer activation, style stock, buyer feedback, and the June 2026 offer |

## Period filter

The sample programs share one month control for fiscal year 2026–27, April through October:

- **FY to date** — April through October
- **Jul–Sep** — the quarter review
- **Apr** through **Oct** — a single month

October is month-to-date through 13 October 2026. Choosing a month, or clicking a bar in the chart, updates the period volume, year-on-year comparison, mix, rankings, activity counts, and takeaways. Growth is green when the period is up and red when it is down.

Exchange and Apparels keep their own filters, because their reports cover a longer range. Brand, model, dealer, style, and survey figures on those tabs stay on the full report even when a month is selected.

Exchange runs from July 2024 through October 2026 (18,702 exchanges):

- **All** — the full report
- **FY 24-25**, **FY 25-26**, and **FY 26-27** — fiscal years, July through June
- Any month in the chart

FY 26-27 is July through October 2026 only. October 2026 is month-to-date through 13 October.

Apparels runs from September 2025 through September 2026 (10,000 units procured, 1,295 retailed):

- **All** — the 13-month review
- **Launch & winter**, **Spring push**, and **Offer window** — the three commercial regimes
- Any month in the chart
