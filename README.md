# Yamaha Projects Dashboard

Performance review for nine Yamaha programs. Open `yamaha-projects-dashboard.html` in a browser. No install or server is required.

The page uses the Yamaha wordmark and a white layout. Influencer uses sample data. The other eight tabs use the figures from their review files.

`index.html` is the original five-program sample pack: Tie-Up, Local Mechanics, Pulse Card, Influencer, and Kizuna.

## Programs

| Tab | What it reviews |
| --- | --- |
| Tie-Up | YRC Eid tie-ups for Apr–Mar 25: 239 footfalls, 96 customers, and the brand list |
| Local Mechanics | Star Mechanic register, sales, leads, top models, dealers, and reward tiers |
| Pulse Card | Yamaha Biker's Care: card sales, stock, staff incentive, and claims |
| Influencer | Creator delivery and campaign results (sample data) |
| Kizuna | Five uploaded videos, views and engagement by place, and the $500 media buying budget |
| Exchange | Bike exchange quantity, brand and model mix, platform, and dealer ranks |
| Apparels | Apparel retail, dealer activation, style stock, buyer feedback, and the June 2026 offer |
| Campus Activation | Campus programs from 2024 through 2026 so far, by year and institute |
| Yamaha Credit | Flagship retail finance since 6 June 2026: customers, delivery, tenure, and models |

## Period filter

Influencer uses the sample month control for fiscal year 2026–27, April through October:

- **FY to date** — April through October
- **Jul–Sep** — the quarter review
- **Apr** through **Oct** — a single month

October is month-to-date through 13 October 2026. Choosing a month, or clicking a bar in the chart, updates the period volume, year-on-year comparison, mix, rankings, activity counts, and takeaways. Growth is green when the period is up and red when it is down.

Tie-Up covers Apr–Mar 25 only.

Local Mechanics covers the Star Mechanic program from January 2024 through May 2026 (3,872 registered, 2,241 sales):

- **All-time** — the full summary
- **Last 1 year** — sales, leads, and Saluto sales for that window

The summary does not include a month-by-month series. Reward tiers, top models, top mechanics, and top dealers stay on the all-time book.

Pulse Card covers Yamaha Biker's Care from December 2025 through September 2026. The board is one period: card sales 12,807, current stock 5,800, dealer staff incentive BDT 88,040, and total claim 175+.

Kizuna lists five uploaded videos. The media buying budget is $500 overall, and total views are shown as 7.6M.

- **All** — the five videos
- **Cox's Bazar**, **Mymensingh**, **Barishal**, **Madaripur**, and **Puran Dhaka** — one video

Exchange runs from July 2024 through October 2026 (18,702 exchanges). Brand, model, and dealer figures stay on the full book:

- **All** — the full report
- **FY 24-25**, **FY 25-26**, and **FY 26-27** — fiscal years, July through June
- Any month in the chart

FY 26-27 is July through October 2026 only. October 2026 is month-to-date through 13 October.

Apparels runs from September 2025 through September 2026 (10,000 units procured, 1,295 retailed):

- **All** — the 13-month review
- **Launch & winter**, **Spring push**, and **Offer window** — the three commercial regimes
- Any month in the chart

Campus Activation covers 2024 through 2026 so far (22+ programs):

- **All**, **2024**, **2025**, and **2026 so far**

Institute visits stay on the full list when a year is selected.

Yamaha Credit starts on 6 June 2026 (10 customers). Delivery, tenure, and model stay on the full book of 10 when a month is selected:

- **All**
- **Jun** through **Oct**, plus **Unspecified**
